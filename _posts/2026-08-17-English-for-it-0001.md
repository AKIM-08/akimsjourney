Being able to speak and understand English is a good thing.

But sometimes, it's not only about knowing English, it's about being able to understand the technical words and expressions in your domain. 

In order to have a better vocabulary in IT English, I decided to start the course **English For IT 1** proposed by [Cisco Networking Academy](https://www.netacad.com)

- [x] The passive voice
- [x] Relative pronouns
- [x] New vocabulary


### New vocabulary


- A Detractor : A customer who had a bad experience with a product

- User Experience (UX) : A personal experience the user has while using the product's interface

- NPS : Which stands for *Net Promoter Score*, a key indicator of customer satisfaction
