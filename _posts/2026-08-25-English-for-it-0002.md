I've been working on a lot of projects lately, so I paused this course for a moment. 

Today, I came back and picked up where I left off. I learned a lot of things—some were brand new, while others were good refreshers.

I focused on **dependent prepositions**, such as:
- Increase **IN**
- Rely **ON**
- Listen **TO**
- Depend **ON**

**Dependent prepositions** are prepositions that naturally follow specific verbs, nouns, or adjectives to connect them to an object.

For example, we cannot say:
- *I'm listening podcasts.* ❌
- *It depends the schedule.* ❌

Instead, we say:
- *I'm listening **to** podcasts.* ✅
- *It depends **on** the schedule.* ✅

There are a lot of dependent prepositions out there. I know I can't memorize all of them at once, but I believe I'll master them brick by brick through regular practice.
